# 🐾 PawKu

### Your Cat's Care, Simplified.

**PawKu** is a mobile-based pet care application designed to help cat owners manage their cats' health and daily needs in a simple and practical way.

## 🐱 About PawKu

Taking care of a cat involves more than providing food and shelter. PawKu helps cat owners keep track of important health information and care schedules in one place.

With PawKu, users can:

* 💉 Manage vaccination schedules and reminders
* 🩺 Schedule and track veterinary check-ups
* 📋 Record and view cat health history
* ⚖️ Monitor cat weight development
* 🔔 Receive automatic care notifications
* 🧮 Calculate vaccination schedules
* 🏪 Discover pet shops and veterinary services
* 📅 Access online booking from participating pet shops

## 🎯 Our Goal

PawKu aims to make cat care **more organized, practical, and accessible** through digital technology.

By bringing pet owners, pet shops, and veterinary services into one platform, PawKu provides a connected ecosystem for better cat care.
